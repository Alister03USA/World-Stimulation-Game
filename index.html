Expedition records

The Jurassic field journal

Face an animal within 65 metres and press F to record it. Keep a safe distance from predators. For marine life, enter the water and dive with C. Your expedition pauses while you read.

Survive your first day. Search the gold supply marker on your map, drink at the blue freshwater spring, and record all three species. Press R to eat a ration. Two more rations become available at the shoreline cache every five minutes. Salt water cannot restore thirst.

These are stylized animals from the Jurassic Period, brought together in a fictional coastal habitat. They do not represent one proven ecosystem. The marine vegetation and survival mechanics are artistic abstractions.

Species references: Allosaurus · Stegosaurus · Cryptoclidus
