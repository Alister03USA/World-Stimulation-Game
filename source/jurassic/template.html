Expedition records

The Jurassic field journal

Face an animal within 65 metres and press F to record it. Keep a safe distance from predators. For marine life, enter the water and dive with C. Your expedition pauses while you read.

Living landscapes

Plants of the Jurassic

Six non-flowering plant lineages shape the coast. These are lineage-inspired reconstructions, not claims that modern species lived alongside every animal shown.

Survive your first day. Search the gold supply marker on your map, drink at the blue freshwater spring, and record all six species. Press R to eat a ration. Two more rations become available at the shoreline cache every five minutes. Salt water cannot restore thirst.

These are stylized animals from the Jurassic Period, brought together in a fictional coastal habitat. They do not represent one proven ecosystem. Plant shapes, animal colors and behavior are reconstructions. Marine vegetation and survival mechanics are artistic abstractions. Detailed Stegosaurus and Brachiosaurus meshes are by Grab3D (CC0).
